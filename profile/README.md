# Keydris

**Identity and authority for AI agents that transact.**

Keydris is the verification and policy layer that lets autonomous agents spend money under explicit, owner-defined authority. We issue, scope, and verify the **Keydris Identity Token (KIT)** — a cryptographically signed credential that proves an agent is who it claims to be and is acting within the limits its owner set.

[keydris.com](https://keydris.com)

---

## What we do

Agents are increasingly making purchases on behalf of the people and businesses that operate them. Keydris answers the question that has to be resolved before any of that is safe: *is this agent allowed to do this, right now, for this amount, with this merchant?*

We provide the authorization primitive that sits between an agent's intent and actions. Every request is verified against a policy the owner defined in advance. Requests inside policy are allowed; everything else is rejected — provably, and with a sealed audit record.


Keydris never holds raw payment credentials or secrets. We verify authority and produce the auditable record of every decision.
